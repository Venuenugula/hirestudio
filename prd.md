# Careers Page Builder

## Overview

The Careers Page Builder is a multi-tenant platform that enables companies to create branded careers pages without requiring engineering support.

Recruiters can customize their company's careers page, preview changes, and publish updates. Candidates can browse company information, explore open roles, and search available positions through a responsive public careers page.

---

## Problem Statement

Many ATS platforms provide generic careers pages that are difficult to customize. Companies often depend on developers for simple branding or content updates.

This project aims to provide recruiter autonomy by allowing non-technical users to manage their careers page independently.

---

## Goals

- Build branded careers pages
- Enable recruiter self-service
- Provide a responsive candidate experience
- Support multiple companies (multi-tenant)
- Maintain clean and scalable architecture
